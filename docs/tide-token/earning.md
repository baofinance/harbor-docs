# Earning TIDE

Users can earn or receive TIDE (and related eligibility) through protocol activities:

1. **Stability Pools**

   - Deposit haTokens into Collateral or Sail Stability Pools
   - Earn collateral yield plus the **75%** revenue share directed to pools (see [Tokenomics](/tide-token/tokenomics))
   - Marks / additional TIDE incentives may apply where allocated

2. **Maiden Voyage Participation**

   - Help bootstrap new markets through [Maiden Voyage 2.0](/maiden-voyage)
   - Earn **Ledger Marks** during the voyage
   - Become eligible for **Yield Share** — up to **~5% of that market’s revenue** (separate from the global 75/25 protocol split)
   - TIDE claim eligibility depends on campaign / snapshot rules in the app

3. **Airdrop / Claim / BAO Swap**

   - Use the [Tide dashboard](https://app.harborfinance.io/tide) to preview snapshot allocation, claim eligibility, and BAO swap balance

4. **Protocol Participation**
   - Provide liquidity for protocol tokens where programs exist
   - Participate in governance discussions
   - Contribute to protocol development
   - Engage with the community

## Revenue Distribution Model

Canonical flow (diagram on [Tokenomics](/tide-token/tokenomics)):

![Harbor protocol revenue and TIDE flow](/img/tide-content-flow.png)

- Protocol revenue = collateral yield + mint/redeem fees
- Above ~$10M TVL → treasury take (~5%+)
- Remaining: **75% stability pools** / **25% buy TIDE**
- Bought TIDE: treasury (to 30%) → POL (to 15%) → burn

- **Growth-Oriented**: Most revenue reinvests into pools and TVL
- **Value Accrual**: Buybacks strengthen treasury, POL, then reduce supply
- **Market founders**: Maiden Voyage Yield Share is a per-market carve-out for early depositors

The TIDE token isn't just an incentive — it's the governance and value-accrual mechanism for Harbor's synthetic asset ecosystem.
