# Earning TIDE

Users can earn or receive TIDE (and related eligibility) through protocol activities:

1. **Stability Pools**

   - Deposit haTokens into Collateral or Sail Stability Pools
   - Earn collateral yield; Marks / TIDE incentives may apply where allocated
   - Participate in securing the protocol

2. **Maiden Voyage Participation**

   - Help bootstrap new markets through [Maiden Voyage 2.0](/maiden-voyage)
   - Earn **Ledger Marks** during the voyage
   - Become eligible for **Yield Share** on that market’s revenue (separate from TIDE emissions)
   - TIDE claim eligibility depends on campaign / snapshot rules in the app

3. **Airdrop / Claim / BAO Swap**

   - Use the [Tide dashboard](https://app.harborfinance.io/tide) to preview snapshot allocation, claim eligibility, and BAO swap balance
   - Historical “12-week points convert at TGE” language is superseded by live claim flows

4. **Protocol Participation**
   - Provide liquidity for protocol tokens where programs exist
   - Participate in governance discussions
   - Contribute to protocol development
   - Engage with the community

## Revenue Distribution Model

:::caution Product confirmation needed
The percentages below are **legacy docs wording**. Align with product before treating as final. See [Tokenomics](/tide-token/tokenomics).
:::

### Historical docs: Pre-$10M TVL (Growth Phase)

- **100% of protocol revenue** reinvested in ecosystem
- **75%** to Stability Pool rewards / market growth
- **25%** to Protocol-Owned Liquidity / TIDE strengthening

### Historical docs: Post-$10M TVL (Transition Phase)

- Treasury takes declining percentage (targeting ≤5% long-term)
- Remaining revenue maintains a growth / POL-style split
- Harbor Treasury maintains ≥30% of TIDE supply for sustainability

### Historical docs: Long-Term (Mature Phase)

- POL reaches target of 15% of circulating supply
- Further buybacks may burn supply
- TIDE becomes deflationary through systematic burns

**Tide app framing:** protocol revenue purchases TIDE → strengthen treasury ownership → build POL → eventually burn.

- **Growth-Oriented**: Early phase maximizes reinvestment into protocol health
- **Value Accrual**: Long-term holders benefit as buybacks, POL, and burns activate
- **Sustainable**: Treasury maintains warchest while minimizing unnecessary dilution
- **Aligned Incentives**: TIDE holders benefit from real protocol revenue and growth

The TIDE token isn't just an incentive — it's the governance and value-accrual mechanism for Harbor's synthetic asset ecosystem.
