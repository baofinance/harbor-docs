# Earning TIDE

Users can earn TIDE through various protocol activities:

1. **Stability Pools**

   - Deposit assets into either Collateral or Sail Stability Pools
   - Earn rewards from 75% of protocol revenue (pre-$10M TVL)
   - Benefit from team-directed incentive allocations
   - Participate in securing the protocol

2. **Maiden Voyage Participation**

   - Help bootstrap new markets through Maiden Voyage
   - Receive TIDE rewards for early support
   - Distribute risk and initial tokens

3. **Early Participation (Points Program)**

   - Earn points during the first 12 weeks post-launch
   - Points convert to TIDE at TGE
   - Rewards genuine users, not mercenary farmers
   - Participate in building initial TVL

4. **Protocol Participation**
   - Provide liquidity for protocol tokens
   - Participate in governance discussions
   - Contribute to protocol development
   - Engage with the community

## Revenue Distribution Model

### Pre-$10M TVL (Growth Phase)

- **100% of protocol revenue** reinvested in ecosystem
- **75%** to Stability Pool rewards
- **25%** to Protocol-Owned Liquidity (building TIDE liquidity)

### Post-$10M TVL (Transition Phase)

- Treasury takes declining percentage (targeting ≤5% long-term)
- Remaining revenue maintains 75/25 split
- Harbor Treasury maintains ≥30% of TIDE supply for sustainability

### Long-Term (Mature Phase)

- POL reaches target of 15% of circulating supply
- 25% allocation switches from POL building to buyback & burn
- ≤5% to treasury operations
- ~75% continues to stability pool rewards
- TIDE becomes deflationary through systematic burns

✅ **Growth-Oriented**: Early phase maximizes reinvestment into protocol health

✅ **Value Accrual**: Long-term holders benefit from buyback & burn mechanism

✅ **Sustainable**: Treasury maintains warchest while minimizing dilution

✅ **Aligned Incentives**: TIDE holders benefit from real protocol revenue and growth

The TIDE token isn't just an incentive — it's the governance, value accrual mechanism, and heartbeat of Harbor's emerging synthetic asset ecosystem.
