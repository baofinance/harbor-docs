tBTC collateral (haUSD)

Status: ✅ Deployed (mainnet USD stack, May 2026)
Chain: Ethereum mainnet (1)
App marketId: tbtc-usd

Mint haUSD and hsTBTC-USD using tBTC as collateral. Shares haUSD with stETH, PAXG, and wBTC.

App source: contracts.mainnetUsd.ts.
